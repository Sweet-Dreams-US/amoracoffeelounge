Our Philosophy · فلسفتنا

Designed for lingering

For us, coffee isn't a transaction. It's a ritual — a moment carved out of the day, served in porcelain, accompanied by a date and a pause.

Inspired by the qahwa houses of Riyadh and the considered cafés of Melbourne, we built a room where the bean is roasted in small lots, the milk is steamed to within a degree, and the light itself was part of the plan — tall arches, cream plaster, mashrabiya shadows moving across the floor all afternoon.

Pistachios are crushed fresh. Rose water comes from Isparta. Cardamom is ground the day it's used.
